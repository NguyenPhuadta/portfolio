SmartHue · Device connection

Removing friction from smart-light connection

Using GA4 to identify where users dropped after device discovery, then proposing a Bridge and Hue BLE Light journey that begins connection automatically after a user starts a scan.

01 · The problem

GA4 showed drop-off after device discovery

Connecting a light was a critical first step in SmartHue. For Bridge and Hue BLE Light, GA4 showed drop-off after devices were found, in the part of the flow where users reviewed a device list and tapped Connect. The data located the drop-off; it did not explain why users left the journey.

In the previous Bridge flow, tapping Connect did not complete the interaction: users then had to press the physical button on the Bridge.

The existing flow also required users to recover when no light was found. Whether the additional selection step itself increased connection time or caused drop-off was not verified with user research.

02 · From data to a product decision

A proposal to reduce manual selection after discovery

I reviewed the connection funnel in GA4 and proposed removing the manual-selection step after discovery. Users still start a scan with Scan Now. When SmartHue finds a supported device, the proposed journey moves directly into connection; the engineering implementation provides the underlying discovery and connection behaviour.

GA4 finding→Remove manual selection→Automatic connection

03 · Before and after

From a device list to direct connection

The redesigned journey removed the device list after discovery. For Bridge, the user moves directly to the physical action required to complete connection.

04 · Clear feedback

Connection feedback designed around engineering states

For Hue BLE Light, I designed feedback that shows the model currently being connected, such as Hue color lamp. The flow presents engineering-provided connection states, including sequential connection when more than one light is found, so progress remains visible without repeated manual selection.

05 · Outcome

Reported team outcome

Following the redesigned connection journey, the team reported increased successful connections and reduced connection time. Public metric definitions, baselines and measurement periods are pending confirmation, so these outcomes are presented qualitatively here.

Successful connectionsIncreased
Connection timeReduced

06 · What I’d improve next

Continue the journey through every detected device

  • Keep the user in the connection journey until every detected device has completed connection, then take them to light controls.
  • Add a factory-reset recovery state for incompatible Hue lights, directing users to the official Philips Hue app when a reset is required.
Future proposal

These directions are not shipped work or reported outcomes.

Limitation

Because I did not have direct access to users, I could not verify why people dropped at the Connect button. The proposed explanation is therefore a hypothesis informed by funnel data, not a confirmed user insight.

To validate this, I would run a usability-testing session with employees who do not work on SmartHue if representative users cannot be recruited.
