SmartHue · Device connection

Removing friction from smart-light connection

Using GA4 to identify where users dropped after device discovery, then proposing a Bridge and Hue BLE Light journey that begins connection automatically after a user starts a scan.

  • RoleEnd-to-end product design
  • ScopeBridge & Hue BLE Light
  • CollaborationPM, Engineering, Marketing & design mentor
  • Outcome+55% connection success · +118% purchase rate

01 · The problem

GA4 showed drop-off after device discovery

  • Signal GA4 located drop-off after device discovery, when users reviewed a device list and tapped Connect.
  • Legacy funnel evidence For Bridge, 64.57% of users who reached FoundDeviceScr_Show then reached the success screen (24.05% from scan). For BLE, the figures were 42.94% and 18.46% respectively.
  • Extra step In the previous Bridge flow, tapping Connect was followed by pressing the physical button on the Bridge.
  • Limitation The data located the drop-off but did not explain why users left; the role of manual selection was not verified with user research.

02 · From data to a product decision

A proposal to reduce manual selection after discovery

  • Decision I proposed removing the manual-selection step after discovery.
  • User control Users still start a scan with Scan Now.
  • Collaboration Once a supported device is found, the flow moves into connection; Engineering implements the underlying discovery and connection behaviour.
GA4 finding→Remove manual selection→Automatic connection

03 · Before and after

From a device list to direct connection

The redesigned journey removed the device list after discovery. For Bridge, the user moves directly to the physical action required to complete connection.

04 · Clear feedback

Connection feedback designed around engineering states

  • Visible feedback The UI shows the model currently being connected, such as Hue color lamp.
  • Multiple lights The shipped flow connects lights in detection order, keeping progress visible without repeated manual selection.
  • System states The interface translates connection states supplied by Engineering into a user-facing journey.

05 · A/B test outcome

Results from the A/B test

In the A/B test, the auto-connect variant was compared with the baseline flow. The variant increased the rate of users reaching the connection-success screen and making an in-app purchase. The team also reported reduced connection time.

Reached connection success17% → 27%A/B test · +55% relative change vs baseline
In-app purchase rate6.1% → 13%A/B test · +118% relative change vs baseline

06 · Post-launch funnel performance

More users completed connection after discovery

  • Measurement Compared matching three-month windows before and after automatic connection was applied. The initial funnel event represents the same connection-flow entry point in both periods.
  • BLE Scan-to-success increased from 18.46% to 25.5%. Among users who found a device, the rate reaching success increased from 42.94% to 46.57%.
  • Bridge Scan-to-success increased from 24.05% to 28.1%. Among users who found a device, the rate reaching success increased from 64.57% to 77.97%.
  • Interpretation The post-launch pattern is consistent with a less interrupted path after device discovery; the before–after comparison alone does not establish causation.

07 · What I’d improve next

Continue the journey through every detected device

  • Completion Keep users in the connection journey until every detected device has completed connection, then take them to light controls.
  • Recovery Add a factory-reset state for incompatible Hue lights, directing users to the official Philips Hue app when a reset is required.
Future proposal

These directions are not shipped work or reported outcomes.

Limitation
  • What I could not verify I did not have direct access to users, so the explanation for drop-off at Connect remains a funnel-informed hypothesis.
  • Next validation Run usability sessions with representative users—or employees outside the SmartHue team if recruitment is not possible.
