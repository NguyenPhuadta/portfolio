### 1. Hero

**Eyebrow**  
Team project · Product Management Challenge 2024 · Top 13/180 teams

**H1**  
Making technical learning more hands-on and connected

**Dek**  
A Katalon Academy concept that explored two gaps in technical learning: turning lessons into guided practice, and getting help without leaving the learning flow.

### 2. The learning gap

**H2**  
Watching lessons was not enough

**Body**  
Interviews and a Katalon Academy survey suggested that learners could lose confidence when they lacked a suitable learning path, timely support, or a clear way to apply what they had learned. Some wanted more hands-on practice than video lessons alone could provide; others struggled to get answers or connect with peers and experts.

The opportunity was to make practice and support part of the learning experience itself.

`[Content image placeholder — Compact research synthesis: need for hands-on practice, timely help and community connection.]`

### 3. Practice Space

**H2**  
Give learners a place to practise, not just watch

**Body**  
Practice Space proposes a library of testing case studies for learners to complete with Katalon Studio. Learners can filter cases by difficulty, testing type, industry and completion status.

Each case provides the brief, instructions and required resources. After practising with Katalon tools, learners submit their result and receive a suggested answer plus related cases to continue practising. Progress history, daily quests and weekly challenges provide additional ways to build a regular practice habit.

`[Content image placeholder — Practice Space wireframes: case-study list with filters, case detail/submission, and results with suggested next cases.]`

### 4. Solution Hub

**H2**  
Bring answers into the learning moment

**Body**  
Solution Hub sits next to a course chapter so learners can ask a question while they are learning. The proposed concept combines Katalon Community resources with AI Search and AI Helper to provide a generated answer and similar previously answered questions.

If the available responses do not resolve the issue, the learner can forward the question to Katalon Community, turning an individual blocker into a new discussion for the community.

`[Content image placeholder — Solution Hub wireframes: question entry beside a chapter, AI/community responses, and escalation to Community.]`

### 5. Learning

**H2**  
Learning support needs to be actionable and timely

**Body**  
The concept reframed technical education around two moments: practising a real task after learning, and resolving uncertainty before it interrupts progress. Practice Space and Solution Hub were designed to support both.
